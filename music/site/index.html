How it's made

Idea: quiet music to leave on for 10 to 20 minutes while playing. 60 BPM, no drums, lots of silence.
Theme: ten piano notes in a narrow range, mostly steps, long holds. It plays in half the cycles only.
Harmony: D major, four chords, two bars each: Dmaj9, Gmaj7, Bm7, Gmaj9. Same cycle the whole time.
Phasing: the melody repeats every 8 bars, the music box every 12, the glass swell every 16, so each pass sounds a little different.
Depth: piano near, pad and bass middle, music box far (dark, wet). 9 s hall, slow echo, wind, tape wobble.
Checks: registers never overlap, no clashing notes. -20 dBFS RMS, seamless 192 s loop. Download
