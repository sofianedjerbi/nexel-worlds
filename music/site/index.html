How it's made

Synth: pure numpy, 48 kHz, in music/compose.py. Run it again to re-render.
Harmony: jazzy 9ths and borrowed chords in F major, a minor bridge in D minor.
Lead: two detuned pulse waves panned wide, slide-in and delayed vibrato.
Low end: sine sub plus a plucky pulse bass, sidechained to the kick.
Space: ping-pong echo and a stereo room, computed circularly so the tails wrap.
Master: look-ahead limiter, -14 dBFS RMS. Download master.wav
