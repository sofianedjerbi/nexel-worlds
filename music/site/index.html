Stems mute or solo the layers, the sum is the master

Sound effects every interaction in the game. Click to play a random variant; it plays over the music if the music is on

In tune: tonal sounds (rewards, UI, chimes, talk blips) use the music's notes, D major pentatonic. The goal chime is the music's own melody cell.
Real models, not beeps: wood and stone ring with their own resonances, gravel and leaves are made of grains, the cow and chicken are shaped voices.
Never the same twice: frequent sounds have 3 to 6 variants. The game can also nudge pitch and pan by where the sound happens.
Levels: loudness is set per category: footsteps quiet, work clear, rewards bright but soft, interface subtle. Made by music/sfx.py.

How it's made

Approach: music that "doesn't explain anything" (C418's words): no story, no climax, one texture that slowly changes.
Harmony: three chords, two bars each: D, G, B minor, G. The same 8 bars for the whole 4.9 minutes.
Melody: two tiny cells in the range of a fifth, repeated exactly. It asks (F#, E, F#, A), then settles (F#, E, D). Nothing new is ever added.
Variation: only who plays it, and what is around it: piano, synth lead, arpeggio, open space. The melody rests in 4 of 11 blocks.
Sound: felt piano through a "cheap mic", detuned analog pad with a slowly breathing filter, Moog-style bass, tape hiss.
Checks: registers never overlap, no clashing notes, loudness per block. Seamless 293 s loop. Download
