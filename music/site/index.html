How it's made

Approach: music that "doesn't explain anything" (C418's words): no story, no climax, one texture that slowly changes.
Harmony: D major in seventh chords, one a bar, all from the key: Dmaj7, Bm7, F#m7, Em7 then A7; Dmaj7, Bm7, A7sus, D6. A harp plays each chord with all its notes together, short, off the beat.
Melody: one head of three notes (A, up to B, back to A), said four times exactly the same. Only the long note it lands on changes: F#, E, F#, D, and each landing comes an eighth early, the way Animal Crossing tunes push. Nothing else is ever added.
Variation: only who plays it, and what is around it: the guitar or an oboe; the band (a bouncing double bass, the harp, a shaker) or open space. A light swing throughout. The melody rests in 4 of 11 blocks.
Sound: every instrument is a recording of a real one, from public-domain libraries (VCSL and VSCO 2 CE by Versilian Studios, the FreePats Spanish guitar): nylon guitar, concert harp, oboe, plucked double bass, violas and cellos held under everything, a shaker. Nothing is synthesised any more except a faint tape hiss.
Checks: registers never overlap, no clashing notes, loudness per block. Seamless 250 s loop at 84 BPM. Download: Fable, Opus
