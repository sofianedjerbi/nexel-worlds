NEXEL WORLDS

The door's sound. First three: "Wood on wood" made faster, as you asked, three ways. Under them the six of before. "Both" opens, waits a second, and closes, as walking through does.

Why six

  • I cannot hear. Five doors made one after the other were each wrong in their own way: a buzz, a lone click, a knock-knock, clicks again, a moan.
  • These are made as the game's other effects are, from code (music/doors.py): struck wood and board, friction, shaped noise. None is in the game yet.
