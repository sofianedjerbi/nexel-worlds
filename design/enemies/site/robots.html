NEXEL WORLDS

A robot of each race, for the AI players: so that at a glance you know who is a person and who is a machine. Shown here before anything is put in the game.

The idea

  • A robot is its race in plate. The head keeps the race's outline (the cat's ears, the bunny's long ears, the duck's bill), so a robot bunny is still a bunny from across a field.
  • What says "machine": steel with a dark edge where the fur was; a dark visor across the face with two lit eyes; the lining of the ears, the cheeks and the collar's bell turned into lamps.
  • Each race has its own light: cat cyan, bunny pink, bear amber, frog green, duck yellow. The lamps are the only bright colour on it, so they also show at night.
  • The plate keeps a little of the player's own colour, as fur does, so two robot cats are not the same grey.
  • They are drawn by the game's own code from the same sheet as the races (web/js/races.js), so every animation exists already: walking, running, hoe, axe, watering can, pickaxe, sword, spear. Clothes go on as on a person.

Not decided yet

  • Nothing in the game wears these. To put them in, the world has to say which players are AI (it does not today), and their robot is drawn in place of their race.
  • Open questions for you: is the visor right on every race, or should some keep a face? Should an AI player choose its race, or get one by chance? Should the lamps blink when it thinks or talks?
