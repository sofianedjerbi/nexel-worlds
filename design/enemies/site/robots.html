NEXEL WORLDS

A robot of each race, for the AI players: so that at a glance you know who is a person and who is a machine. The look is the "tin cat", chosen from eleven: the race as it is, in steel.

The idea

  • A robot is its race in steel, and nothing else is changed: the same head, the same face, the same dark eyes. Nothing covers the face.
  • Grey bolts where the blush was. The lining of the ears and the bell at the collar are lit.
  • Each race has its own light: cat cyan, bunny pink, bear amber, frog green, duck yellow.
  • The steel keeps a little of the player's own colour, so two robot cats are not the same grey.
  • Every animation exists already, and clothes go on as on a person.

Voices

A robot talks with the same recorded syllables as a person, on the same beat, each in a voice of its own from its name; only the sound is made a machine's. Pick one: it goes into the game as it is here (web/js/robot_voice.js).

In the game

  • An AI player is drawn as the robot of its race, in the world and in the list of who is here. People are drawn as before.
  • An AI player's race comes from its name, so they are not all the same.
  • An AI player talks in the robot voice chosen above; people keep their babble.
