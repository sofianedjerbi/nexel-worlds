How it works

Nothing is redrawn. Each of the player's 64 frames (standing and walking, four directions) is read once: where the skull starts, where the collar is, where the feet are. Clothes are painted onto those places, so they follow every bob and step.
Five parts. Head sits on the skull, over or between the ears. Top is the collar and chest. Pants are the two rows under it. Shoes are the feet. The effect is drawn around the whole player.
Same rules as the sprites. Flat colours with one shade, an outline in a darker tone of the cloth, never black. Armor also recolours the player's own outline where it covers it.
Tiers. Cloth and leather are everyday; iron and gold are armor; the moss set is made from Old Mossback's shell plates and the sporecap hat from a sporecap.
Effects are computed. Sparkles, fireflies and leaves orbit; flame and frost follow the player's outline; spores and flowers trail behind only while walking.
Every piece is also an item. The small square on each tile is how it looks in a bag, 16 by 16 like the game's own items; the slots under the strip show the five you are wearing. An effect is worn as a charm: a gem on a cord.
