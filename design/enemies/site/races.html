How they are made

Same body, same frames. Each race is the cat's own sheet with its head rebuilt: the ears come off, the top is rounded, and the race's own ears, eyes or muzzle go on, frame by frame. So every race already has every animation the cat has.
Known by the outline. Tall ears, round ears, eyes on top: each reads from its silhouette alone at game size, from the front, the side and behind.
Same style. Flat colours with one shade, and an outline in a darker tone of the body rather than the cat's purple.
Clothes still fit. The collar, chest and feet are untouched, so tops, pants, shoes and charms work as they are. Hats sit on the skull; the bunny's ears would need to poke through them.
Tints still work. The game gives each player a body colour. The second button shows the races under those tints: the shape, not the colour, tells them apart.
The perks are only suggestions. One small thing each, so a race is a taste and not a class. They are not built; say which to keep, change or drop.
