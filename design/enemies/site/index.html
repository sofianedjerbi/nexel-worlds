How they are made

Every frame is computed. Nothing is drawn by hand: each mob is a few shapes moved by a formula, so its 8 frames flow like the player's. That was what worked for the wisp and the wyrm.
Five ways of moving. One waddles, one hops, one tunnels, one flies, one trudges. No two share a body plan, a size or a colour family, so each is known by its outline alone.
Built like the game's own. Small, round, flat colours with one shade, an outline in a darker tone of the body (never black), the same soft purple shadow, 8-frame moves at 80 to 110 ms, a slow breath at rest.
Every action warns first. A held pose of about half a second (squat, open mouth, trembling mound, wings held high, fists up), then one fast frame, a hold, a recovery. The bigger the hit, the longer the warning.
Each one asks for a different answer. Keep your distance, chase it, pave your floor, roof your coop, wait for the opening. Only the cairn does real damage, and only if you mine beside it.
Rare by design. One sporecap in a forest, one hush per night, one cairn per mountain. None would spawn until you say so.
