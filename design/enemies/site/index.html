New enemies

designs for Megastructure

In the world, at game size each one walks a loop, so you see it turn through all four directions

How they are made

Different bodies: no two share a shape. A long four-legged boar, a tall two-legged bird, a wide crab, a legless snake, a winged moth seen from above, a person-shaped scarecrow, and a fish that is only a fin.
Four directions: each sheet is laid out like the game's character.png: rows are down, up, left, right; columns are the walk frames, then the action frames. Right is left mirrored.
Style: the game's Sprout Lands look: a dark outline in the creature's own colour, flat fills with one shade, tiny dark eyes, a ground shadow. Animals are drawn in profile when seen from the side, like the cow.
One idea each: it charges, stabs, guards, poisons, puts out your lights, walks when unwatched, or waits in the water. Each has an answer a player can learn.
Source: hand-placed pixels, one character per pixel, one file per enemy in design/enemies/roster/. build.py makes the sheets and this page.
