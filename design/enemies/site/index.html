New enemies

designs for Megastructure

In the world, at game size each one walks a loop, stops, does its action, and walks on

How they are made

The newest three move like the player: the wisp and the wyrm are computed frame by frame (nothing drawn by hand but the wyrm's heads), and the stag walks on generated legs with its head nodding once a step.
Different bodies: no two share a shape. A long four-legged boar, a tall two-legged bird, a wide crab, a legless snake, a winged moth seen from above, a person-shaped scarecrow, and a fish that is only a fin.
Four directions: each sheet is laid out like the game's character.png: rows are down, up, left, right; columns are idle, walk, then the action. Right is left mirrored.
Walks match the game: the boar, heron, scarecrow and snake walk in 8 frames at about 90 ms, like the game's cow, chicken and player. The boar's body stays level like the cow's; seen from the front, bodies rise a pixel as a foot lifts, like the player. Side-view legs are generated, so every frame is consistent.
Attacks warn first: every action is wind-up, strike, hold, recover, each with its own length. The wind-up is held for about half a second so a player can read it; the strike is one fast frame.
Idle and shadows match too: a slow one-pixel breath and a blink, and the same soft purple oval under the feet as the cow and chicken. The scarecrow alone stands dead still.
The snake is computed: its body is a wave drawn from a phase, so the bends travel smoothly from head to tail.
Source: one file per enemy in design/enemies/roster/, one character per pixel; frames are built from parts (body, legs, head). build.py makes the sheets and this page.
