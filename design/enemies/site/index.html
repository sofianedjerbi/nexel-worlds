How they are made

Style: the Sprout Lands look the game already uses: 16 px sprites, one mauve-brown outline, flat fills with a single shade, dot eyes, a soft ground shadow. The boss is 32 px, like the cow.
Palette:
taken from the game's own sheets, plus stone, moss and fire tones already used by its generated tiles.
One idea each: every enemy has one silhouette and one behaviour a player can learn: it rolls, it hides, it swoops, it lashes, it steals, it burns. None is just "a thing that walks at you".
Fits the sandbox: they act on the world, not only on health: the Pilfer robs chests, the Emberling burns what you built, Cairn breaks blocks. Fences, light, water and doors are the answers.
Source: hand-placed pixels in design/enemies/sprites.py, one character per pixel. build.py makes the sheet (enemies.png) and this page's data.
Not in the game yet: these are designs. Tell me which to keep, change or drop, and I will animate them further and wire them in.
